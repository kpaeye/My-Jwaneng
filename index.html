🧠 Did You Know?
Jwaneng is home to one of the richest diamond mines in the world.
The name "Jwaneng" means "place of small stones" in Setswana.
Debswana’s Jwaneng Mine is called the “Prince of Mines”.
The town was built specifically for mining operations.
Jwaneng contributes significantly to Botswana’s GDP.
