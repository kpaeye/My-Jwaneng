How TownConnect Works

From community voice to meaningful action.

01 — SPEAK

Raise the Matter

Report an issue, submit a suggestion, make a request or provide feedback.

02 — ROUTE

We Route It

The submission can be directed to the appropriate authority, organisation or community structure.

03 — RESPOND

Action & Response

The responsible team can review the matter and provide an appropriate response.

04 — TRACK

Follow Progress

Use your reference number to follow the progress of your submission.

Your town. Your voice. Your connection.

TownConnect is designed to create a digital bridge between the people of Jwaneng and the structures responsible for making the town work.

Residents, businesses, institutions, community leaders and public authorities can ultimately use one connected platform to communicate, collaborate and respond to community needs.
