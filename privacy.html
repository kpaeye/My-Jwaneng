MyJwaneng Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MyJwaneng collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when you use our platform.

Information is used to: improve platform functionality, display listings and content, communicate updates, and maintain community services.

MyJwaneng uses secure cloud services (including Supabase) to store data. We take reasonable steps to protect data from unauthorized access or misuse.

5. Public Content

Some content on MyJwaneng (such as business listings, events, and news) may be visible publicly. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal data.

6. Third-Party Sources

Some information, images, or listings may originate from publicly available sources or third-party submissions. MyJwaneng does not claim ownership of such content.

7. Cookies & Analytics

The platform may use cookies or basic analytics tools to understand user activity and improve user experience.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may only be shared when required for platform operation, legal compliance, or user-requested services.

9. Your Rights

You may request correction or removal of your data by contacting MyJwaneng support.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of MyJwaneng means you accept the updated version.

11. Contact

For privacy-related concerns, contact MyJwaneng via the official contact page.
