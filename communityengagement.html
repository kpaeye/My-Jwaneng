How Leadership Connect Works

A simple path from community voice to community action.

01 — SUBMIT

Tell Us

Submit your issue, suggestion or community request through the platform.

02 — REVIEW

We Route It

Your submission can be directed to the relevant authority or community structure.

03 — ACTION

Response & Action

The relevant team can review the matter and provide an update or action.

04 — TRACK

Follow Progress

Use your reference number to follow the status of your submission.
