💡 Did You Know? — Jwaneng
Jwaneng is a planned town in Botswana's Southern District.
The town has a strong community identity shaped by people from many backgrounds.
Jwana Game Park is associated with the wider Jwaneng area and its natural environment.
Jwaneng's story is closely connected to Botswana's diamond industry — but town life extends far beyond it.
Jwaneng has local businesses, sporting activities, community organisations and events.
The Kalahari landscape gives Jwaneng a distinctive natural setting.
